Mara discovered the lighthouse keeper's logbook, yellowed membranes damp with the sea. A single entry read, 'The light was on. They did not turn.' That night as she lit the oil lamp, her heart thudded. Looming stairs echoed her descent, each step crunching beneath her. Nervous breaths fogged in the cool air. Would they come? Through the fogged glass, the light seemed steady, passive. Her hands trembled as she turned the key in the lock.
